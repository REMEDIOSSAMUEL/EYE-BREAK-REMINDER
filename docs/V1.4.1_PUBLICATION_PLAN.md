## Personal laptop test after publication

1. Open Eye Break Reminder. In Settings → About, confirm **Installed version: 1.4.0**.
2. Note a few existing settings and history entries to compare after the update. With your approval, exit the app via its tray menu and copy the Eye Break Reminder data directory at %LOCALAPPDATA%\EyeBreakReminder to a separate backup folder. Check that the copy contains your settings.ini and history.dat, if present. Reopen the app after the copy.
3. Go to Settings → About and click **Check for Updates**. Confirm the offer shows current version 1.4.0 and available version v1.4.1.
4. Keep **Preserve my settings (Recommended)** selected and click **Download & Install** when ready. Observe the progress and the **Ready to install** message with the successful SHA-256 check. The production updater requires a valid production manifest before offering the update. The ready screen reports the payload digest result.
5. If you choose to proceed, click **Install Update**. Watch the original app close and the successor relaunch. Close the completion dialog if prompted.
6. In Settings → About, confirm **Installed version: 1.4.1**. Check the settings and history entries from step 2. On the Timer tab, observe the countdown advance during normal running hours.
7. In Settings → About, click **Check for Updates** again. Confirm latest stable v1.4.1 and **Eye Break Reminder is up to date**.
